Bringing Nature's Golden Goodness to Every Home

Bringing Nature's Golden Goodness to Every Home

At Golden Milk Turmeric, we believe that quality begins at the source. Our turmeric is cultivated in fertile farms, carefully harvested, hygienically processed, and packaged using food-grade standards. Our mission is to provide pure turmeric products that support healthy lifestyles while maintaining authenticity and natural goodness.

Our 11-Step Farm to Package Cycle

From the rich soils of our organic farms to the final global delivery, trace each sequential phase of our meticulous farming and cold-manufacturing process.

Step 01
Scroll photos

Land Preparation & Soil Management

The journey of premium turmeric begins with fertile, well-drained soil. Fields are carefully prepared by ploughing and enriching the soil with organic manure to create ideal growing conditions.

Soil testing Organic manure application Field leveling Bed preparation
Step 02
Scroll photos

Seed Rhizome Selection & Planting

Healthy and disease-free turmeric rhizomes are selected as planting material. These rhizomes are planted at the beginning of the monsoon season to ensure optimal growth.

Selection of quality rhizomes Disease inspection Proper spacing Timely plantation
Step 03
Scroll photos

Crop Growth & Field Care

Throughout the growing season, the crop receives regular irrigation, nutrient management, and weed control to ensure healthy plant development.

Irrigation management Weed control Organic nutrition Crop monitoring
Step 04
Scroll photos

Harvesting Mature Turmeric

After 7–9 months of cultivation, mature turmeric rhizomes are harvested carefully to preserve quality and maximize yield.

Manual harvesting Rhizome collection Sorting Transportation to processing unit
Step 05
Scroll photos

Cleaning & Washing

Freshly harvested turmeric is thoroughly washed to remove soil, stones, and other impurities.

Water washing Dirt removal Quality inspection Pre-processing cleaning
Step 06
Scroll photos

Boiling / Curing Process

The cleaned turmeric is boiled under controlled conditions. This curing process enhances color, aroma, and shelf life while reducing raw odor.

Controlled boiling Uniform curing Color development Quality control
Step 07
Scroll photos

Drying Process

The cured turmeric is dried naturally under sunlight or through hygienic mechanical dryers until the desired moisture level is achieved.

Sun drying Moisture reduction Quality monitoring Safe handling
Step 08
Scroll photos

Polishing & Grading

Dried turmeric fingers are polished to improve appearance and graded according to size, color, and quality standards.

Surface polishing Size grading Quality segregation Final inspection
Step 09
Scroll photos

Grinding Into Fine Powder

Selected turmeric fingers are finely ground using modern milling equipment to preserve natural color, aroma, and curcumin content.

Fine grinding Particle size control Hygiene maintenance Quality assurance

Scroll photos

Quality Testing

Every batch undergoes rigorous quality checks for purity, color, moisture, and safety before packaging.

Purity testing Moisture analysis Microbiological testing Batch approval

Packaging & Distribution

The finished product is packed in food-grade packaging to maintain freshness and delivered to customers across domestic and international markets.

Food-grade packaging Batch coding Warehouse storage Nationwide & export shipping
