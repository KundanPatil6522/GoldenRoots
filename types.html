Turmeric Varieties & Uses

Not all turmeric is created equal. Different soils, elevations, and farming climates yield distinct properties. Discover the unique therapeutic, culinary, and beauty treasures of the golden root.

Therapeutic / Wellness

Lakadong Turmeric

7-12% Curcumin (High Curcumin Content) Meghalaya, India Active Index Score: 6 (Supreme Grade)

Why It's Special: Known as one of the world's richest natural sources of curcumin.

Best For:
  • Wellness & Supplements
  • Golden Milk
Gourmet & Commercial

Salem Turmeric

3.0% - 4.5% Curcumin Tamil Nadu, India

Why It's Special: Deep color and strong aroma.

Best For:
  • Cooking
  • Spice Manufacturing
  • Export
Everyday & Culinary

Erode Turmeric

2.5% - 4.0% Curcumin Tamil Nadu, India

Why It's Special: Famous globally for quality and vibrant yellow color.

Best For:
  • Household Cooking
  • Food Industry
Bold & Premium

Rajapuri Turmeric

3.5% - 4.5% Curcumin Maharashtra, India

Why It's Special: Large rhizomes and excellent yield.

Best For:
  • Turmeric Powder
  • Food Processing
Gourmet & Export

Alleppey Finger Turmeric

5.0% - 6.0% Curcumin Kerala, India

Why It's Special: Bright orange color and high demand in international markets.

Best For:
  • Export
  • Premium Powder
